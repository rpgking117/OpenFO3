OpenFO3 1.0 - open-source Fallout 3 engine
by RPGKING The Mad Modder

You need your own copy of Fallout 3 (Steam, GOG or disc). No game files
are included. OpenFO3 doesn't change your Fallout 3 install, settings or
saves.

This is version 1.0 and it isn't finished. Bugs will be common, including
crashes and stuck quests.

The programs aren't code-signed, so Windows or your antivirus may warn you.
If you see "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".

After installing, run OpenFO3 from the Start menu. Saves go to
Documents\My Games\OpenFO3 and are kept if you uninstall.
